The process, animated

Watch the gate judge.

An action is proposed. The eight witnesses judge it under the rubrics, each from its own domain. Dissent stays on the record. A critical finding blocks the action. Then the verdict is sealed the way SoulCradle seals it: a hash computed from the verdict and the block before it. In production that block is written to the sealed record and kept. Your record stays in your building

SoulCradle is delivered software. It runs in your environment, your cloud, your servers, or air-gapped, and the judgment record it produces is generated and kept there. Mythara Labs does not receive your data.

When an audit comes, your team calls the record from your own system, by subject and period, through authenticated access. Any ledger you export verifies on its own, every seal re-checked, so your auditor can confirm the record was not altered after the fact, without our involvement. On termination the record remains yours. It was never anywhere else.

The architecture

Compliance is the foundation. Benevolence is the measure.

The SoulCradle is the whole mechanism, and compliance is its foundation. The rules come first: governance frameworks, versioned rubrics, controls mapped the way a global governance maps them. Woven through those rules is the Benevolence Engine, so a judgment is never only legal or only kind, it is measured on both. Underneath, the Witnesses, the matrix, and SERE judge and guard in a sealed record. Above, the egos, the pantheon of gods, speak and want out loud. The record itself is the discipline: every judgment is sealed in a hash chain. The chain proves a record is unaltered. It does not prove the record is true, and Mythara says so herself, in her own foundation documents.

The rules: compliance, mapped globally

Mythara's governance core maps controls against the frameworks the regulated world runs on: the HIPAA Security Rule, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, SOC 2, and ISO 27001, organized in a multi-jurisdiction matrix. The work is readiness mapping, controls checked against each framework and cited to the rule version they were judged under. That mapping is the product, and it is stated as exactly what it is.

The Witnesses and the benevolence matrix

Eight assessor-witnesses judge every story and every proposed action against a benevolence matrix. Each one scores from its own domain. The change in benevolence is the gravity of the matter. They abstain when their domain is not engaged, they fail closed when evidence is missing, and disagreement is preserved in the record, never averaged away. Three findings can block outright: deception under Eros, deception under Hermes, and disproportionate harm under Nemesis.

SERE, the guarding core

SERE keeps the perimeter and the record. It detects, quarantines, profiles, and logs. Its defense is the record itself: tamper-evident, hash-chained, and written so that a quiet edit breaks the chain and shows. SERE comes in three directions: Legacy for training and triage, Desktop guarding a single machine, and Enterprise, still in development. In every direction the rule holds: SERE guards, and it never attacks.

The pantheon, the egos

The gods are the personalities: Icarus hosts, and the witnesses speak as themselves, from their own temperaments, arguing the story in front of them. The matrix judges in numbers nobody hears. The gods react in words everybody hears. Scoring is never spoken. Personality is never faked from a template.
