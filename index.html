Product 1

The Witness Gate

An open-source accountability layer for AI agents. Eight fail-closed, evidence-fed witness assessors evaluate an agent’s proposed action before execution.

Gate before execution

The proposed action is assessed first. Missing evidence is treated as a flag, not a pass.

Sealed judgments

Every judgment is sealed and written to a tamper-evident, hash-chained attestation record.

Dissent preserved

Assessor disagreement remains visible in the record. It is preserved, never averaged away.

The honest contract

The chain proves that a record is unaltered, not that the record is true. The Witness Gate is not emotion detection.
