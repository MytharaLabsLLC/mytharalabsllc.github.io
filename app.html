Mythara News for Android

The daily Mythara News editions, print and radio, in one app. Eight reporters read the morning news, then tell you the truth the coverage is hiding.

Download the app (APK, 26 KB)

Version 1.0 · Android 8.0 or newer · Signed by Mythara Labs LLC

How to install

  1. Tap the download button on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file, mythara-news.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser for this one install. The app is signed by Mythara Labs LLC.
  4. Tap Install, then open Mythara News.

The app loads the latest editions from mytharalabs.com each time you open it, so it is always current. Also available soon on the Samsung Galaxy Store.
