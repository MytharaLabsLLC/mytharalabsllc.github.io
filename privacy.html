Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 | Mythara Labs LLC, Denver, Colorado.

The short version

Our software is delivered software. It runs in your environment, on your machines, and the records it produces stay there. Mythara Labs does not receive, host, or process your operational data. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties, except as required by law or valid legal process.

What our software collects

Nothing, for us. SoulCradle, the Witness Gate, and our bot products run locally in your environment. They do not phone home, and no telemetry is sent to Mythara Labs. The judgment records and data the software creates are yours, stored where you installed it.

What this website collects

This website is a static site. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no contact forms. We set no tracking cookies and run no advertising or analytics trackers of our own.

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and browser information for security and operational purposes, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Site chat: the chat at the corner of this page runs in your browser. It does not save or send us your email address, and it does not transmit your conversation to Mythara Labs.
  • Search engines: this site carries verification tags for Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. These confirm to Google and Microsoft that we operate this site; they do not track visitors for us.

What happens when you buy

Checkout is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment information directly; your card number never reaches Mythara Labs and is never stored by us. Stripe shares with us the details we need to complete and support your order, such as your name, email address, and what you purchased. Stripe's handling of your information is governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

After payment, your product is delivered by download, and we use your email address to deliver it and to provide support.

Email you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it to answer you and to support products you bought. Support material you voluntarily send us stays in our mailbox for as long as we need it for the support record and our business records. On license termination you may request deletion of support material you sent us, as described in our Terms of Service.

What we never do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share personal information with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not receive or store your operational data from our software.
  • We do not store payment card numbers.

The only exception: we will disclose information when required by law or valid legal process, such as a subpoena, warrant, or court order.

Your choices

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you (in practice: your emails to us and your order records), ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will act on your request. Order records may be retained where the law or our tax and accounting obligations require it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date. Material changes affecting customers will also be notified by email at least 30 days before taking effect, consistent with our Terms of Service.
